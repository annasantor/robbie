*/ To test on real hardware (while you have connected the arduino of the robotic manipulator to your computer) leave the ros2_control xacro file as it is now.
If you want to have a simulation on gazebo, change this part:

  <ros2_control name="ArmHardware" type="system">
    <hardware>
      <plugin>my_robot_controller/ArmHardwareInterface</plugin>
      <param name="serial_port">/dev/ttyACM0</param>
      <param name="baud_rate">9600</param>
      <param name="use_serial">true</param>
    </hardware>


To this :

  <ros2_control name="GazeboSystem" type="system">
    <hardware>
        <plugin>gz_ros2_control/GazeboSimSystem</plugin>
    </hardware>
